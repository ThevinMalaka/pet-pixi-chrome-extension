# Pet Pixi — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 16 November 2026_

Pet Pixi is a Chrome extension that shows pixel pets at the bottom of your web pages. It has **no accounts and no ads**, and it **does not read or collect your browsing**.

## What is stored on your computer
Everything Pet Pixi needs to work is saved only in your browser's local extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`) on this computer:
- your pets (species, names, bond level, hunger and happiness values, adoption date, today's care progress)
- your settings (pet size, pixel style, night mode, free roam, sites where pets are hidden)
- Photo Pets you created — either a small pixel-art version or a small cut-out of your photo (resized to about 160 px), depending on the look you chose. Your original photo is not kept.
- your Pet Pixi Pro license key, if you bought one
- a random install id used for the anonymous statistics below

"Hide pets on this tab" is remembered only until the tab is closed or Chrome restarts.

This data is not synced to other devices or Chrome profiles. Uninstalling the extension deletes it.

## Anonymous usage statistics (you can turn this off)
To understand how many people use Pet Pixi and which pets they like, the extension sends **at most one small message per day** to our statistics server (hosted on Railway). It contains:
- a random install id generated by the extension (not linked to your name, email, Google account or device)
- the extension version and your browser's language setting
- how many pets you have and which species (for example "cat", "dog")
- whether Pet Pixi Pro is active, your pixel style setting and your daily-visit streak

The server works out the **country** your request came from (by looking up the connection address in a country table, then discarding the address) and records it with the day. **It does not store your IP address.** When you uninstall, Chrome opens a goodbye page that tells the server this install id was removed.

You can switch this off at any time: **Pet Pixi popup → Settings → Share anonymous usage stats**, or on the welcome page before your first pet. When it is off, nothing is sent and no uninstall page is opened.

Statistics are only seen by the developer, kept for as long as they are useful to improve Pet Pixi, and never sold or shared for advertising. Because they are anonymous, we usually cannot tell which record belongs to you; turning the setting off stops all collection.

## Photos
When you create a Photo Pet, your photo is processed entirely inside the extension on your computer. **It is never uploaded anywhere.** Only the small finished pet (pixel art or a small cut-out) is kept, on your computer.

## Web pages
Pet Pixi runs on web pages only to draw your pets on top of them. It does not read, collect or transmit page content, web addresses, browsing history, or anything you type.

## Purchases (Pet Pixi Pro)
Pet Pixi Pro (an optional one-time purchase) is sold through Dodo Payments, the merchant of record, which processes your payment and email under [its own privacy policy](https://dodopayments.com/privacy-policy). The extension contacts `live.dodopayments.com` only to activate your license key and re-check it once a day (only the key and its Dodo Payments activation id are sent), so a refunded key stops unlocking Pro. When you open the checkout from the extension (with statistics on), the random install id is attached so purchases can be counted per country. We do not receive or store your name, email or card details from Dodo Payments.

## Children
Pet Pixi does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new "Last updated" date.
